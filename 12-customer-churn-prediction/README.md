# Customer Churn Prediction

Predicts which telecom customers are about to cancel their subscription, using IBM's **Telco Customer Churn** dataset (7,032 customers after cleaning; 26.6% churned).

## Verified result (executed notebook)
- **Logistic Regression: 80.8% accuracy** ✅ best
- Random Forest: 78.8%

## Run it
Open `customer_churn_prediction.ipynb` — it downloads the dataset itself and runs end to end. If the mirror is ever unreachable, the notebook falls back to a clearly labelled synthetic dataset with the same shape.

## Why it matters
Keeping a customer costs far less than winning a new one. A churn model lets a business spot at-risk customers early and act — a discount, a call, a better plan — before they leave.
